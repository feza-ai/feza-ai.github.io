Quantitative research

Wolf

A software foundation for researching market strategies and evaluating them before capital is at risk.

What exists

Reusable Go services, training and backtesting components, risk and portfolio primitives, broker integration code, and SwiftUI tooling.

Where it’s going

The current roadmap focuses on autonomous research for COIN shares and options, independent evaluation, and controlled Alpaca paper trading.

Research & development

No sustainable trading edge is established. Evaluation and recovery gaps remain; paper activation is gated, and live trading is not authorized.

Machine learning infrastructure

Zerfoo

A Go ML framework for embedding model inference, training, and serving in applications.

Inspect the public repository ↗

What exists

GGUF model loading, GPU acceleration, an OpenAI-compatible serving API, and built-in tabular ML and time-series forecasting.

A broader starting point

General-purpose ML infrastructure gives a buyer room to explore applications beyond trading, from embedded AI to tools for Go developers.

Open source · Apache 2.0

The public code is available under its existing license. Any company-owned rights, repositories, or related assets included in a sale must be agreed during diligence.
